# The World — 22 November 2026

A single-image summary of the world's news on 22 November 2026, drawn as the front page of a newspaper called THE WORLD. One glance carries the whole day: the missiles over Poland, the quake in Peru, a probe a light-day out, and the politics, courts and markets that defined it.

## What's inside

- Russian missiles land in Poland; NATO calls the strike a violation.
- A 6.8-magnitude earthquake shakes Peru; Lima residents flee to the streets.
- NASA's Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth, a historic milestone.
- Al-Shabaab claims hundreds dead in an ambush on Somali troops.
- Norway's supreme court voids climate-disputed oil licences.
- Democrats retake the U.S. Congress in a midterm victory.
- Christine Lagarde resigns at the ECB; the EU hunts a new central bank chief.
- Kenya discharges its last Ebola patient as the DRC toll passes 5,300.
- Taiwan bans short selling after stocks fall 17.5 percent in a week.

## How to run

Run the program with python3 main.py. It copies entry.png into an /output directory, creating the folder first when it is missing, then verifies the copy by size and SHA-256.

The entry image is entry.png, 683x1024.
